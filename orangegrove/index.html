The house

Room to breathe, minutes from the city.

Orange Grove sits in the settled, leafy part of Gaborone North — close enough to Phakalane to be convenient, far enough from the noise to actually rest. It suits people here for work as easily as families here for a weekend.

Rooms are finished simply and kept properly: air conditioning, a smart TV, a private bathroom, and a tea and coffee station in every one. Breakfast is halal, à la carte, and cooked fresh each morning.

Outside there's a pool, a garden that people actually sit in, secure parking on the property, and someone on the gate around the clock.
